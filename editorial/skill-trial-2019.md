## What this trial shows

- Four letter-verifiable mechanisms: two hidden answers, two containers and one reversal. The homophone is semantic/phonetic and **not** mechanically certified. The count is six clues in total.
- The two hidden-word clues are valid, but relying on two in six clues would be excessive in a finished puzzle. The composer should generate alternatives before selecting the whole-grid set.
- Short answers permit concise surfaces; these results **do not demonstrate** the ability to clue long entries such as SUSCEPTIBILITY or ACCOMPLISHMENTS.
- The HOMOPHONE clue for OURS is weak despite a defensible British pronunciation; it should be revised or replaced.
- Do not claim that the creative or editorial quality of these clues has been independently verified.
